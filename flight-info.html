Flight & Travel Information

This section provides essential information for travel to and from Spain. Please read carefully to ensure a smooth journey.

📍 Meeting Point

Location: McDonald’s, Meridian Business Park, Meridian East, Leicester, LE19 1WZ

A coach transfer to the airport will be provided. Students must arrive on time and remain with the group.

🧳 Luggage Allowance

Cabin Bag

  • Maximum size: 40 × 20 × 25 cm
  • Must fit under the seat
  • No liquids over 100ml
  • Laptops and valuables must be in hand luggage

Checked Baggage

  • 20kg maximum weight
  • Clothing and toiletries only
  • Bags will be checked in at the airport

📝 Travel Day Rules

  • Follow staff instructions at all times
  • Keep passport secure
  • No alcohol or inappropriate behaviour
  • Remain with the group during travel

🔁 Return Travel

Return travel details will be confirmed during the placement. Students must follow the same behaviour expectations on the journey home.
